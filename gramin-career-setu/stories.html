ఒక విద్యార్థి వర్క్‌షాప్‌లో పాల్గొని, తమ కథను పంచుకోవడానికి లిఖితపూర్వక అనుమతి ఇచ్చిన తర్వాత మాత్రమే మేము దానిని ప్రచురిస్తాము.

Before a story is added here

ఒక కథను జోడించే ముందు

  • The student took part in one of our workshops, and we can name the date and village.
  • The student has signed a consent form allowing their name, photograph and outcome to be published.
  • The outcome described actually happened, and we can say how we know (admission letter, appointment order, exam result).
  • The student is told they can ask us to remove the story at any time, and how to do that.
  • Nothing in the story claims this platform caused the outcome where the student says otherwise.
