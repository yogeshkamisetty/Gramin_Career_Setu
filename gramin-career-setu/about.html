About Gramin Career Setu

A Bridge To Your Career

Gramin Career Setu is a community service initiative to empower rural youth with the right career guidance, opportunities, and resources — in their own language, for a brighter future.

target

Our Mission

To empower rural youth with career guidance, practical skills, and verified opportunities to build sustainable futures.

visibility

Our Vision

A rural India where every student has equal access to the right guidance, technology, and resources to achieve their dreams.

handshake

Our Values

  • Inclusivity & Access
  • Youth Empowerment
  • Community First
  • Sustainability
favorite

Why It Matters

Rural youth often lack awareness of emerging careers. We bridge that gap with offline tech and peer mentorship.
