# High Demand

Software Engineer

Design, build and maintain software that powers websites, apps and systems. Work with modern programming languages to solve real-world problems.

₹ 3.5L – ₹ 15L / year Average Salary
Very High Job Demand
★ 4.6 / 5 Career Rating
✦ IT & Software ✦ Future Ready ✦ Remote Work Possible ✦ APSSDC Free Training
description

Overview

Software Engineers design, develop and maintain software applications. They work with programming languages, databases, cloud tools and modern frameworks to solve real-world problems.

how_to_reg

Eligibility

  • 10+2 with Mathematics (Any Stream)
  • Bachelor's Degree in B.E / B.Tech / B.Sc (CS/IT) or related field
  • Good logical and problem solving skills

• 1–3 Years: ₹ 6L – ₹ 10L / year
• 5+ Years: ₹ 10L – ₹ 15L+ / year
