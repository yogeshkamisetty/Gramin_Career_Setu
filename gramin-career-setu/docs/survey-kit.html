Survey day guide

Print one copy of this page, the tally sheet, and enough form pages for two forms per participant (one BEFORE, one AFTER). Each form page holds two forms; cut along the dashed line.

Steps

  1. Explain the survey and read the consent line aloud. Anyone may decline.
  2. Give each person a code: 01, 02, 03… They write this code on both forms. Do not write names.
  3. Collect the BEFORE forms before you teach or show anything.
  4. Run the workshop.
  5. Give the AFTER forms. Help anyone who cannot read by reading the questions aloud, without suggesting an answer.
  6. Match forms by code. Discard any person who does not have both forms.
  7. Fill the tally sheet the same day.

Rules that keep the data valid

  • Do not change an answer, fill a blank, or round a count. A blank answer is not a YES.
  • Keep the paper forms. They are your proof if an evaluator asks.
  • Get permission from the school head, sarpanch, or organiser before the session. If participants are under 18, ask for that permission in writing.
  • Write in your report that this is a small sample from one location, with no control group, and that answers are self-reported.
